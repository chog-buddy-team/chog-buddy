## Platform

web

## Users
- **Primary: Chogathon 2026 judges.** They see Chog Buddy for the first time, often in Judge Mode without a Chog, and must understand fast what it is and why the NFT matters.
- **Secondary: Chog Genesis holders.** They run the desktop pet, sign in with the wallet that holds their Chog and visit the Park to see their Chog's Soul.

## Product Purpose
Chog Buddy turns a Chog Genesis NFT into a pixel desktop pet (Mac and Windows). It reacts to what the owner is doing (Category minutes such as coding or video), has a personality from its traits (Archetype), keeps a private local Diary, and grows a public onchain Soul on Monad testnet. The web app (`web/`) is the backend plus three public pages:
- `/`, the landing page
- `/connect`, the wallet sign-in page opened by the desktop app
- `/park`, Chog Park

Success: a judge gets "the NFT is essential, and it's alive" within seconds; a holder signs in without confusion.

## Positioning
The pet is the NFT. No Chog means no pet (Judge Mode is a labelled demo). The Soul, personality and "My NFT skin" are bound to the token ID. The Soul grows from real days, and the diary never leaves the computer.

## Operating Context
- `/connect` opens from the desktop app via the tray or the first-launch dialog. Its flow:
  1. Connect a browser wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Backpack).
  2. Sign a gasless SIWE message.
  3. The page hands the session back to the app on `127.0.0.1`.
  The user then returns to their desktop.
- `/park` opens from the app tray ("Open Chog Park") or a shared link. It lists Chogs active in the last 7 days, with Soul axes, level, mood line and a daily focus leaderboard.
- Judges arrive from the hackathon submission.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js 16 App Router on Vercel free tier with Neon Postgres. $0 budget, no new dependencies without reason.
- `/connect` logic (SIWE, nonce, loopback callback, validation, busy and done states) must stay behaviourally identical.
- The Park never shows wallet addresses, Diary or chat text, or any Category besides focus minutes (coding + design).
- Chain: Chog Genesis on Monad mainnet (chain 143). ChogSoul on Monad testnet.

## Brand Commitments
- Faithful to the official Chog art: purple spiky hood (#6c4ab6), cream face (#f6e9c7), pink blush and nose (#f48caa), dark ink (#2a1842). The in-app pet is a pixel sprite of the canonical Chog.
- At home in the Monad ecosystem.
- The privacy promise is visible: the diary stays on your computer, and only Category minutes leave it.

## Evidence on Hand
- Pixel sprite atlas renders (generated by the app; e.g. `atlas.png` in app userData when `CHOG_DUMP_ATLAS=1`).
- Chog NFT images via the Pinata gateway (`https://chog-genesis.mypinata.cloud/ipfs/`).
- Demo Chog #5 with an onchain Soul (Level 7).
- No testimonials, user counts or press exist. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. The NFT is essential: every surface ties back to a specific Chog.
2. Alive, not static: the Chog has a voice and a mood.
3. Private by default, public by choice: the Soul is public, and the diary never is.
4. Explain in one glance for a first-time judge.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast in light and dark. Works on mobile (16px gutter, no horizontal scroll). The wallet flow is usable by keyboard.
