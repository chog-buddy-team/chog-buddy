# Surface brief: Chog Buddy web (landing, /connect, /park)

Scope: three public pages, one world. Landing = Persuade (judges first, holders second). /connect = Operate (one task: sign in with the wallet that holds the Chog, hand the session to the desktop app). /park = Persuade/Experience (see that Chogs are alive and grow onchain Souls). Constraints: /connect logic unchanged; Park shows no addresses, diary, chat or non-focus categories; privacy promise visible.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every Chog is a sticker you peel off the web and stick onto your desktop. Refuses the category default: dark crypto hero with gradient glow over a card grid.
OWN-WORLD: A gridded vinyl backing sheet in Chog hood purple carries the page; Chogs are glossy die-cut stickers with thick white kiss-cut borders and soft lift shadows; only four inks (hood purple, cream, blush pink, ink); fat rounded display lettering with a white outline and ink shadow; small mono labels like sticker-sheet print codes; holo foil only on the leaderboard strip.
STORY: A judge understands in one glance that the pixel Chog is the NFT, alive on your desktop, with a private diary and a public onchain Soul; they visit the Park or get the app. A holder peels their Chog onto their desktop via /connect.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Landing: giant outlined "Chog Buddy" lettering across the sheet, a large classic Chog sticker peeling into place right of it, one hook line, primary "Get Chog Buddy" sticker-button plus "Visit Chog Park"; three small skin stickers scattered with handwritten-size labels for Reacts / Private diary / Onchain Soul. Connect: one big sticker card centred on the sheet, corner peeled while waiting, flat when done. Park: lettering header, holo leaderboard strip, then the album grid of NFT stickers with mono labels and Soul bars.
FORM: kiss-cut sticker sheet, position 7 of my ordered list; seed key 691d8b30. Raises: limited inks (from risograph), uniform label grid per sticker (from sneaker box stacks), outlined fat lettering via paint-order stroke (from highlife sleeve), exact-length Soul bars (from labanotation), state as a mark not a hue: peeled corner = waiting, flat = done (from cutting bench).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
